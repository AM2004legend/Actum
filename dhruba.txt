Hi Dhrubaparna! Here's what we need from you for Actum (our offline Android app). It's two jobs: get Actum running and tested on your phone, then train a small AI model for it on your Mac. Your coding agent does most of the work; you just need to set things up and keep an eye on it.

*What you need first*
• Your Android phone: Android 10 or newer, about 3.5 GB free, and a USB cable to your Mac.
• USB debugging on: Settings → About phone → tap "Build number" 7 times → Developer options → turn on "USB debugging". Plug in and tap "Allow" on the phone.
• Your Mac (M3) with Android Studio installed (it brings the Android SDK and adb), and about 5 GB free.
• A Hugging Face account. Open these two pages and click "Agree" on the Gemma licence (you must be logged in):
  https://huggingface.co/litert-community/gemma-3-270m-it
  https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-270m-it

*Good to know*
• Having Breadcrumb on your phone is fine. Actum is a completely separate app; neither touches the other. Don't uninstall anything.
• Actum never uses the internet. It downloads nothing itself; your agent copies the AI model files onto the phone over the cable (about 3 GB, takes a few minutes).
• The first time you tap the mic, allow microphone access. Voice needs an English speech pack: Settings → System → Languages → on-device speech recognition. If the app says it can't listen, that's why.
• If you try the diary, the password you set can't be recovered. Use one you'll remember.
• Your phone gets warm while Actum's AI is reading. That's expected. Please note it if it gets hot.
